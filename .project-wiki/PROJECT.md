# Group 7 — Humanoid Robots (Electrolux)

The investment-bank case study asks how humanoid robotics is performing overall, which technology segments are growing or fading, and which emerging companies are attractive to acquire or partner with for the client **Electrolux**. This is a course simulation, not a real commission.

**Source of course requirements:** `PW_0_kickoff.pdf`, Innovation Management 2026/27, 6 October 2026, provided in the project context (not copied to this repository). Slides 4–9 define the case and data collection; 11–16 define the analytical stages; 17–21 establish deliverables; 24 gives evaluation criteria.

**Deadline: Sunday 20 December 2026.** Deliver (1) a 20-slide main deck, with <=10 figures/tables and a technical appendix; (2) a reproducible GitHub pipeline, with submission tag `v1.0-submission` and commit hash; (3) a pitch video <=10 minutes, with every team member speaking, and pitch slides. The deck needs references, contributions, and generative-AI-use disclosure.

Patent applications are evidence of R&D, not directly of market performance. The intended unit is the DOCDB patent family, and the course requests earliest **priority** year rather than the current export's earliest filing year. See [open questions](requirements/open-questions.md).
