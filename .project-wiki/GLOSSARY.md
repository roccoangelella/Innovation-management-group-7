# Glossary

**DOCDB family:** patent-family grouping intended to count an invention once. **CPC:** Cooperative Patent Classification. **TIP:** EPO Technology Intelligence Platform. **PATSTAT:** international patent database queried from TIP. **Earliest priority year:** intended event-time for patent landscaping, distinct from earliest filing year. **Forward citations:** later documents citing a patent; right-truncated for recent cohorts. **Magic Quadrant:** present-versus-future firm score visualization adapted from Gartner-inspired patent portfolio analysis.
