# Analysis brief

The required storyline: sector technologies, volume and evolution; CPC technology mix and geography; patent versus secrecy; concentration and entrants; segment growth; mandatory PEST analysis; present company score using influence, volume, success, globalization; original future score with temporal validation; patent-based Magic Quadrant; and an evidence-supported Electrolux acquire-or-partner recommendation with risks (kickoff slides 11–16).

Agreed screening approach (project discussion, 28 October 2026): generate a broad global candidate corpus, preserve non-English patents, translate abstracts, and assess **abstract-only** relevance using an unfine-tuned calibrated decision model. Avoid deterministic keyword prefilters and title-only judgments. Evaluate an initial **100 stratified candidate** sample. A real humanoid patent abstract is a possible relevance reference, not yet a frozen specification.
